Randomizer Hint Viewer: Broadcaster Guide


First-time setup

1. Go to hintviewer.com and log in with your Twitch account
2. Upload your spoiler log JSON file using the upload button or use the create button to enter your own hints
3. The spoiler log is saved to the server; viewers can now see the ??? placeholders in the extension


Before a run

Configuring seed settings

After uploading a spoiler log, click the Seed Settings button to review and adjust the settings for your seed. The settings are automatically extracted from the spoiler log, but you can change them before saving. Click "Save to Viewers" to publish the settings so viewers can see them in the extension. These settings help viewers understand the rules of the seed without needing to ask in chat.


During a run

Revealing a hint

Use the eye button next to any hint location to reveal it to viewers. Hints with the same text across locations are revealed together automatically. Use the Reveal All / Hide All buttons to bulk-toggle entire sections.

Marking a hint as completed

Once a hint is revealed, a checkmark button appears next to it. Click it to mark the hint completed and it will appear struck through for both you and viewers. Un-revealing a hint automatically clears its completed state.

Setting the hinted item

Once a hint is completed you can hit the edit button and select the item the hint was hinting to.

Using the shop tracker (DK64)

While playing DK64, click the Shop Tracker button to open the shop tracker panel. Use it to track which moves and items you have purchased from Cranky, Funky, and Candy across each level. This helps you and your viewers keep track of what has been collected during the run.

Navigating levels

Use the carousel arrows or the Quick Nav sidebar to jump between level pages.


What viewers see

Viewers see ??? for unrevealed hints and the hint text for revealed ones. Completed hints appear with a strikethrough and reduced opacity. The extension auto-refreshes every 60 seconds; viewers can also hit the refresh button to update immediately.


Starting a new run

Upload a new spoiler log to replace the current one and reset all hints.
